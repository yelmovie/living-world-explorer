## 영상 프롬프트 (영문, 이미지→영상)

Real brightfield microscope footage, locked-off camera, no zoom, no pan. The single Paramecium swims slowly and smoothly forward toward the upper right, its rounded front end leading, gently rotating along its long axis as it moves, the way real ciliates do. The fine cilia around its whole edge beat in fast rippling waves, clearly visible along the outline. Inside the cell, food vacuoles drift slightly and the star-shaped contractile vacuole near the back end slowly fills and then contracts once. The cell travels about one third of the frame width over the clip so its start and end positions are clearly different, and it stays fully inside the circular microscope field the whole time. Tiny debris particles and small green algae cells drift very slowly with the water, a few slightly out of focus. Soft even illumination, natural photomicrograph colour, subtle focus breathing, realistic motion speed, no motion blur trails. No text, no labels, no scale bar, no camera shake, no cuts, no new organisms appearing, the paramecium keeps the same shape and size.
