## 영상 프롬프트 (영문, 이미지→영상)

Real brightfield microscope footage of living Spirogyra filaments in a drop of pond water, locked-off camera, no pan. The long green filaments stay in place and only sway very slightly as the water gently moves, drifting a few pixels together without bending sharply. The spiral ribbon-shaped chloroplasts keep their exact helix shape; inside the cells there is very subtle slow cytoplasmic movement and tiny granules shimmer faintly. Small debris particles and one or two tiny single-celled algae drift slowly across the background with the water current. Halfway through, the fine focus is turned slowly so the front filament becomes perfectly sharp while the blurred filament behind stays soft, like a student adjusting the microscope. Soft even illumination, natural photomicrograph colour, realistic slow motion speed. No text, no labels, no scale bar, no camera shake, no cuts, no new organisms appearing, the filaments do not break, grow or change shape.
